Why we started

Most brands aren't underperforming. They're unmanaged.

Talk to enough founders and the same picture appears. One agency on Amazon ads. Someone's cousin doing Flipkart. Quick commerce handled by whoever had a contact. Nobody owning the catalogue. Nobody watching inventory. Everyone reporting on their own slice, and the slices never adding up to the number in the bank.

That's not a talent problem. It's a structure problem, and it's fixable by one team holding the whole thing. That's the entire idea behind MarketFild.

One team

Marketplace, quick commerce, ads and creative under one roof and one invoice.

One invoice

Scoped pricing, never a percentage of your ad spend.

One inbox

The person who reads your account is the person who answers you.

How we behave

Three habits, held tightly.

Operations before advertising

Catalogue, inventory, pricing and account health are where most of the value sits. Ads are the last layer, not the first.

Quick commerce as its own craft

Onboarding, assortment, dark store planning and forecasting, run by people who know it isn't marketplace work with faster delivery.

Plain language, in writing

A monthly note you can actually read, including what went wrong. No dashboard link standing in for an explanation.

Fit

Who we're good for, and who we're not.

Good fit

  • Brands already selling, with a working product and a messy account
  • Brands entering quick commerce who want it run properly, not bolted on
  • Brands tired of coordinating three agencies who blame each other
  • Sellers abroad who need an operations team in their working hours

Poor fit

  • Anyone wanting a guaranteed return quoted before we've seen the account
  • Anyone who wants spend scaled this week regardless of the catalogue
  • Brands looking purely for the cheapest hourly rate
  • Anyone who'd rather not hear what isn't working

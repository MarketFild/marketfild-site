The first 90 days

Slow at the start, on purpose.

  1. Week 1 — Read

    Catalogue, inventory, pricing, ad structure, account health, and everywhere the reporting disagrees with itself.

  2. Week 2 — Prioritise

    Three columns: change now, leave alone, needs more data. The middle one is where most damage gets done elsewhere.

  3. Weeks 3-6 — Fix

    Catalogue, listings and inventory first. Changes go in groups so every one of them can be measured on its own.

  4. Weeks 7-12 — Scale

    Spend increases only once the pages can convert it. Reviewed in writing every month from here on.

Principles

Four things we don't bend on.

Fix the shelf before you buy the traffic

Advertising multiplies whatever your listing already does. If the page converts badly, more spend buys you a bigger loss faster. Catalogue and inventory come first, every time, even when it's the less exciting invoice.

One change at a time, logged

Changes go in as attributable groups with the date and the reason recorded. When something moves we can tell you what caused it. When something breaks we can undo exactly that.

No percentage of ad spend

Charging a cut of spend pays us to spend more of your money. We price on scope. If the right answer is to cut your budget in half, we can actually say it.

We tell you when we're wrong

It goes in the monthly note. An agency that has never reported a mistake is either not looking or not telling you, and both cost you more than the mistake would have.

Month to month

What working together feels like.

A weekly call

Thirty minutes. What moved, what's blocked, what we need from you. Cancelled without ceremony in a week with nothing to say.

A written monthly review

Numbers with the reasoning attached and next month's priorities. Something you can forward to someone who wasn't on the calls.

One person who knows you

You're not handed to a junior after onboarding. Whoever audits your account is whoever runs it in month twelve.

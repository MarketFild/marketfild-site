The honest version

Renting a store is easy. It is also permanent.

Hosted platforms are genuinely good products, and for some brands they are the right answer. But you are buying convenience on a lease: a monthly fee, more monthly fees for the apps that make it usable, and a platform that owns the code your business runs on.

A custom build costs more once. Then it stops costing. The only recurring bill is your own server, which is a few dollars a month, and it stays yours whether or not you ever speak to us again.

What you get with an owned store

  • No platform subscription, ever
  • No percentage taken from your orders by a platform
  • No app subscriptions to add basic features
  • Anything you can describe, we can build. No "the theme doesn't support that"
  • Your data stays in your database, exportable any time
  • Page speed built in rather than fought for

What you take on

  • A higher cost up front instead of spread over years
  • A server that needs someone responsible for it, which is what the care plan is
  • Four to six weeks to build rather than a weekend of templates
  • It is worth it if you plan to run the store for years, and it is not if you are testing an idea for three months

Questions

The ones worth asking.

Why not just use a hosted platform?
Because you rent it forever. The subscription, the apps that make it usable, and a platform that owns the code your business runs on. Hosted platforms are good products and sometimes the right answer, particularly if you are testing an idea. If you plan to run this store for years, owning it works out cheaper and you can build anything you want rather than anything the theme allows.
Who owns the site when it is finished?
You do, completely. Domain in your name, server on your account, code handed to you at launch. If you stop working with us tomorrow the store keeps running and you keep every file. We are not a middleman on anything.
What if something breaks at 2am?
That is what the care plan covers: monitoring, backups, patches and fixes. Being honest about the trade-off, a hosted platform has a team watching servers around the clock and we are a small team. What we give you instead is someone who knows your specific store rather than a support queue.
Can you work with my existing store?
Sometimes. If you are on a hosted platform and want to move, we will tell you honestly whether it is worth it for your volume. Sometimes the answer is to stay where you are and fix the storefront, and we will say that even though it is the smaller job.
Do you also run marketplaces?
Yes, that is our main work. Amazon, Flipkart and Walmart and quick commerce. Most brands we build sites for are selling on marketplaces too, and the two are better run together than apart.
What do you need from me to start?
Your product list with prices and variants, whatever photography you already have, and about two hours of your time in week one. Everything else is ours to chase.

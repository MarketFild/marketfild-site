Questions

The things people ask first.

How much does it cost?
It depends on channel count and catalogue size, so quoting a number here would be guesswork. What we can promise is that the audit is free and you get a fixed price before anything starts. No percentage-of-spend model, because that pays us to spend your money.
Do you lock us into a contract?
No. We work on a rolling monthly agreement after an initial 90 days, which is the shortest honest window for this kind of work to show anything. Leave with 30 days' notice.
Can you guarantee a return on ad spend?
No, and anyone who does before seeing your account is selling you something. We'll give you a realistic range after the audit, with the assumptions written down so you can argue with them.
We already have an agency. Is the audit still useful?
Yes, and you're free to take the findings straight to them. Plenty of brands do. We'd rather you fix the account than sign with us for the wrong reasons.
Do you work with brands outside India?
Yes. We manage Amazon and Walmart accounts for sellers in the US, UK and Canada. Calls and reporting run on your working hours.
How quickly can you start?
The audit takes about a week from the moment we have account access. Execution starts the week after the plan is signed off.

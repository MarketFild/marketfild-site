The work

What we actually do, week to week.

Catalogue and listings

Titles, bullets, backend keywords, image sequence, A+ modules and variation families. We fix the parent-child structure first, because a broken family quietly splits your reviews and your ranking across listings that should be one.

Inventory and fulfilment

Restock forecasts by SKU, storage and placement decisions, and alerts before you run out. A stockout on a ranked listing costs more than the lost orders, because the ranking doesn't come back on its own.

Pricing, buy box and offers

Price bands you set, monitored continuously. Coupon and promotion structure, bundle and multipack maths, and competitor movement watched on your top SKUs rather than the whole catalogue.

Advertising

Campaign architecture built so spend can be attributed. Separate budgets for separate jobs, so your most efficient campaigns aren't starved by a bigger one sharing the same pot.

Account health

Daily monitoring of policy flags, listing suppressions and performance metrics. Appeals written from scratch against the actual violation, not pasted from a template that gets auto-rejected.

Claims and recovery

Lost and damaged inventory, overcharged fees, wrong dimensions and returns never restocked. This is money already owed to you, and most accounts have never claimed it.

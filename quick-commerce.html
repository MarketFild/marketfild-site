Why it's different

The same catalogue will not work twice.

Marketplace buyer

  • Searches, compares, reads reviews, decides later
  • Buys larger packs, less often
  • Ranking built over weeks
  • Stockout hurts ranking
  • National warehouse coverage

Quick commerce buyer

  • Buys smaller packs, far more often
  • Visibility bought and negotiated

Platforms

Three that matter. We run all three.

Between them they hold the overwhelming majority of quick commerce in India. Anything beyond these three is usually a distraction until you are winning on them.

Blinkit

The largest network and the deepest metro coverage. Favours established, high-frequency products with real replenishment demand.

  • Onboarding and commercial terms

Zepto

The most aggressive on new categories. Often the easiest entry point for beauty, personal care and newer brands.

Swiggy Instamart

Strong in southern metros and wide on SKU variety, which makes it a good second launch for regional brands.

The work

From first pitch to steady fill rate.

Onboarding and commercials

Brand deck, category fit, margin structure and listing fees. Getting on the platform is straightforward. Getting on at terms you can live with for the next year is the actual work.

Assortment and pack sizes

Your marketplace hero SKU is often the wrong lead here. We pick pack sizes and price points for an impulse basket, not a considered purchase, and cut the SKUs that will only dilute your shelf.

City and dark store coverage

Where you launch decides sell-through more than your creative does. We pick clusters against your category's real density instead of spreading thin across a whole metro and looking slow everywhere.

Forecasting and fill rate

A forecasting error here shows up the same week, as a stockout or as wastage. We hold weekly numbers with the platform and move before a gap becomes a delisting conversation.

Visibility and ads

Banner slots, category positioning and platform advertising, planned around the days your category actually converts rather than spread evenly through the month.
